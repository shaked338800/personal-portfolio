About Me

I've completed all academic requirements for a B.Sc. in Computer Science at HIT (Holon Institute of Technology), and most of what I know about building software comes from building it.

My hands-on work is mainly full-stack: React and TypeScript on the frontend, Node.js and Express on the backend, and PostgreSQL with Prisma for data. I've built REST APIs with authentication and validation, deployed my projects, and in recent projects added LLM-powered features, including retrieval (RAG) and tool calling.

What interests me most is the backend side: how APIs, data models and AI services fit together into something reliable. I'm looking for a junior role where I can learn from an experienced team and grow as a software engineer.
